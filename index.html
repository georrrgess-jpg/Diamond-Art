How to play

  1. Pick a gem

    Choose a numbered gem from the tray at the bottom.

  2. Tap matching numbers

    Spots with that number light up. Tap one to place a gem. To fill several, start your drag on a lit spot and sweep across. Press and hold any spot to switch to its gem.

  3. Zoom in for detail

    Drag with one finger anywhere else to move around. Pinch, scroll or double-tap to zoom. Undo takes back your last stroke. Finished artworks are saved to My Collection, where you can play them again.

  4. Stuck?

    Find shows you a spot that still needs the selected gem. Progress saves automatically.
