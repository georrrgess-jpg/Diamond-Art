How to play

  1. Pick a gem

    Choose a numbered gem from the tray at the bottom.

  2. Tap matching numbers

    Spots with that number light up. Tap one, or drag across several to fill them in one stroke. Press and hold any spot to switch to its gem.

  3. Zoom in for detail

    Pinch, scroll or double-tap to zoom. Use two fingers, the Move tool or Space + drag to look around. Undo takes back your last stroke.

  4. Stuck?

    Find shows you a spot that still needs the selected gem. Progress saves automatically.
